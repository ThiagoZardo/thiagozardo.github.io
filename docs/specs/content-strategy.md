# Content Strategy

## North star

In less than 20 seconds, a technical recruiter should understand that Thiago owns problems across application code, backend, mobile, data, integrations, infrastructure and production.

## Message hierarchy

1. Role and end-to-end scope.
2. Current production responsibility at Unex.
3. Four concrete engineering cases.
4. Troubleshooting model and collaboration evidence.
5. Grouped tools used to deliver that work.
6. Contact.

Large editorial headlines use outcome-led language instead of generic claims. The hero establishes application-to-production scope; subsequent calls reinforce systems thinking, real ownership and collaboration without repeating the same promise.

## Social proof

- Preserve all four verified LinkedIn recommendations.
- Present one recommendation at a time in a carousel with automatic rotation, manual previous/next controls and direct selection.
- Pause rotation during pointer or keyboard interaction and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Keep recommendation wording concise while preserving the meaning and attribution of the source feedback.

## Voice

- Direct, calm and evidence-based.
- First person where ownership matters.
- No “passionate about technology”, student framing or inflated seniority.
- Prefer verbs: built, structured, automated, investigated, separated, guided, corrected.
- Use qualitative impact where no verified measurement exists.

## Case template

`Context / Problem → What I owned → Engineering outcome`

Confidential details are abstracted. Security is described only as identification and correction of issues in applications and deployment processes.

## Project review

- Current Unex work: KEEP as the basis of the current-focus summary and selected engineering cases, without visually ranking the company above the rest of the career.
- Sandbox Environment: KEEP as a flagship case.
- Deployment Pipeline: KEEP as a flagship case.
- Production Troubleshooting: KEEP as a capability case.
- Engineering Automation with Claude Code: KEEP as a differentiator.
- Financial integrations: mention inside scope, not as an unsupported standalone case.
- Unex, Mosyle, SolarMarket and Control 361: KEEP with equal visual weight in the professional experience sequence. Each receives context, applied contribution, technology scope and an external link. Selected current engineering work remains a separate section.
- Academic/basic public projects: MOVE TO LABS or REMOVE; not shown on the landing page.
