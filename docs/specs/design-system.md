# Design System

## Direction

Editorial engineering portfolio: warm paper surface, deep ink, precise blue accent, hairline rules, generous whitespace and compact technical annotations. The design avoids terminal imitation, neon, gamer cues and oversized logo walls.

## Tokens

- Canvas: `#f4f1ea`
- Surface: `#fbfaf7`
- Ink: `#111827`
- Muted ink: `#5d6470`
- Border: `rgba(17, 24, 39, .14)`
- Accent: `#1d4ed8`
- Accent dark: `#173c9a`
- Signal: `#d95c35`
- Maximum reading width: 76rem
- Body radius: 18px; compact radius: 999px

## Typography

System-first sans serif for performance. Large, tightly tracked display text; readable 1.6–1.75 body line height; uppercase monospace-style labels only as subtle metadata.

## Components

- Sticky translucent masthead.
- Hero with editorial copy and an “operating range” evidence panel.
- Experience block with asymmetric hierarchy.
- Numbered case studies, alternating density rather than repetitive cards.
- Equal-weight professional company sequence, preceded by a compact current-focus summary.
- Troubleshooting trace rendered as a connected diagnostic path with layer-level evidence and Observe → Isolate → Validate phases.
- Grouped stack columns.
- Minimal quote cards and contact footer.

## Motion

Only short opacity/vertical reveals and hover state changes. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables smooth scrolling and transformations.
